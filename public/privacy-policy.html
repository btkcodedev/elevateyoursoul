Privacy Policy for Elevate Your Soul

Effective Date: June 24, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Elevate Your Soul (hosted at https://elevateyoursoul.web.app/) collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website.

1. Information Collected via Firebase Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand how our website is used. This service collects data such as:

  • Usage data (e.g., pages visited, time spent on pages).
  • Device information (e.g., browser type, operating system).
  • IP address and general geographic information.

This information is processed to analyze user trends and improve the quality of our content. For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit the Google Privacy & Terms page.

2. Cookies and Identifiers

Firebase services use cookies and anonymous identifiers to recognize your device. These technologies help us understand how you interact with our site. You can control the use of cookies at the individual browser level. If you reject cookies, you may still use our website, but your ability to use some features or areas may be limited.

3. Your Rights and Opt-Out

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of any personal data we may hold. Because we use Google Analytics for Firebase, you can opt-out of analytics tracking by:

  • Using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Adjusting your device settings (for mobile users).

4. Data Retention and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through our website. Data collected via Firebase is subject to Google's data processing terms. We retain this data only as long as necessary to provide our services and satisfy legal or reporting requirements.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
